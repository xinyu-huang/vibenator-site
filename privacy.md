# Gershwin Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

Gershwin is a music library application that runs entirely on your Mac. It has
no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers operated by us. We do not collect,
store, receive, or transmit any personal information about you.

## What stays on your Mac

Your music files, the library database Gershwin builds from them, your play
history, and every setting you change are stored only on your own computer,
inside the library package you create (usually in your Music folder).
Gershwin never uploads them anywhere.

## What Gershwin sends to other services

To identify your music and fetch cover art, Gershwin sends queries to the
following third-party services. These queries contain information about your
music files — such as track titles, album and artist names, durations, disc
identifiers, and acoustic fingerprints computed from the audio — and never your
name, email address, account identifiers, or the contents of your files.

- **MusicBrainz** (musicbrainz.org) and the **Cover Art Archive**
  (coverartarchive.org) — release, recording, and cover art lookups.
- **AcoustID** (api.acoustid.org) — acoustic fingerprint lookups, used to
  identify tracks whose tags are missing or wrong. The fingerprint is a compact
  numeric summary of the audio; the audio itself is never sent.

When you ask the built-in Search assistant about an album, Gershwin also looks up
background about that album and the people credited on it. Those queries contain
the album title and artist names, and go to:

- **Wikipedia** (wikipedia.org) — articles about the album, the work, and the
  performers, conductors and composers credited.
- **DuckDuckGo** (api.duckduckgo.com) — an instant-answer summary, used when
  Wikipedia has nothing or as a second source.
- **Apple iTunes Search** (itunes.apple.com) — release facts such as date, genre
  and label, from Apple's public catalogue search.

These three are used only by the Search assistant. If you never use it, they are
never contacted.

Each of these services receives your device's IP address as an ordinary part of
making an internet request, and each has its own privacy policy governing what
it does with the requests it receives. Gershwin sends no identifier that would
let them connect those requests to you across sessions.

## Your local network

If you play music to a UPnP/DLNA renderer, Gershwin discovers devices on your
local network and serves audio to the device you select. This traffic stays on
your network and reaches no one else. macOS will ask your permission the first
time; if you decline, the rest of the application continues to work.

## Microphone

If you turn on View ▸ Show Music Sound Level, Gershwin uses your Mac's built-in
microphone while music is playing to show roughly how loud it is in the room.
Only a sound level is calculated; nothing is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
macOS asks your permission the first time, and the microphone is never used
unless you turn this on.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

There are none. Gershwin contains no analytics framework, no advertising, no
crash reporting service, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about how you use
the application is reported to us or to anyone else.

## Children

Gershwin is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone,
including children.
