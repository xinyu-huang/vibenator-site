# Gershwin

A music library for your Mac. Gershwin scans the folders you choose, identifies
each album against MusicBrainz and AcoustID, finds duplicates across formats and
copies, and plays everything bit-perfect — to your Mac's output, a USB DAC, or a
UPnP/DLNA renderer on your network.

**Gershwin Remote** is a separate free app for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It browses
your library and controls playback on your Mac from anywhere in your home, and the
music keeps playing on the Mac. It needs Gershwin 1.1 or later, works on your home
network only, and uses no account and no cloud. Remote Control is off by default:
turn it on in Playback ▸ Remote Control, then pair each device with a six-digit code.
