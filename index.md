# Gershwin

A music library for your Mac. Gershwin scans the folders you choose, identifies
each album against MusicBrainz and AcoustID, finds duplicates across formats and
copies, and plays everything bit-perfect — to your Mac's output, a USB DAC, or a
UPnP/DLNA renderer on your network.
