# Sample music

Free music to try Gershwin with. Gershwin offers both downloads the first time it
opens with no library; they are listed here too, to use without the app.

- [Small sample (about 6 MB)](https://github.com/xinyu-huang/vibenator-site/releases/download/sample-music/Gershwin-Sample-Music-Small.zip):
  one album, *The Shade from Our Trees* by Josh Woodward, as small HE-AAC files.
- [Full sample (about 250 MB)](https://github.com/xinyu-huang/vibenator-site/releases/download/review-sample-music/Vibenator-Review-Sample-Music.zip):
  two freely licensed albums as MP3 files, with their credits inside the zip.

*The Shade from Our Trees* (2019) is by [Josh Woodward](https://www.joshwoodward.com/),
used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
licence. The full-quality album is at
[archive.org](https://archive.org/details/JoshWoodward-TheShadeFromOurTrees).
The small sample is a lower-quality copy.
