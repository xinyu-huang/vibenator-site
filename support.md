# Vibenator Support

**Contact:** [xinyu.huang@gmail.com](mailto:xinyu.huang@gmail.com). Please include
your macOS version and Vibenator's version (Vibenator ▸ About Vibenator).

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
- The Search assistant's natural-language answers use Apple Intelligence, which
  needs macOS 26 and Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Search still works
  with keywords.

## Common questions

**Vibenator says it has no permission to read a folder.**
The Mac App Store version can only read folders you have chosen yourself.
Click **Grant Access…** in the banner and confirm each folder, or add it again
from the Sources button with **Add…**. Vibenator remembers each folder once you
have confirmed it.

**My UPnP/DLNA renderer doesn't appear.**
macOS asks for permission the first time Vibenator looks on your local network.
If you declined, turn it back on in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local
Network, then restart Vibenator. The renderer and your Mac must be on the same
network.

**Some albums stay unmatched.**
Not every release is in MusicBrainz, and Vibenator would rather leave an album
unmatched than attach it to the wrong release. You can pick a release by hand in
the **Inspect/Fine-tune Enrichments** window.

**Does Vibenator change my files?**
No. Vibenator reads your music files and never modifies, moves or deletes them.
What it learns is kept in its own library, and excluding a duplicate only hides
it from the library.
